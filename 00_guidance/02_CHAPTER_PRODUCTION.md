# 02 — CHAPTER PRODUCTION PROCEDURE

## Execution Position

Run after **01 — AUTHORITY, BASELINE & RESEARCH PROCEDURE** and before **03 — IMMERSIVE PROSE & DIALOGUE PROCEDURE**.

This file controls chapter construction and repository synchronization. Detailed immersion rules live only in ProCEdure 03; detailed historical/research rules live only in ProCEdure 01; post-production synchronization is finalized in ProCEdure 04.

## Execution Order

1. Author instruction
2. Authority and dependency reconciliation
3. Baseline / targeted research
4. SCEne planning
5. Drafting
6. ProCEdure 03 immersion pass
7. ProCEdure 04 post-production audit
8. Synchronize and verify
9. Deliver

---

# 1. PRE-PRODUCTION

## Author Instruction

Identify:

- explicit story events;
- requested character interactions;
- requested decisions or developments;
- requested locations/time;
- explicit overrides;
- constraints that must not change.

The latest explicit Author instruction has priority when it clearly replaCEs or adds to older compatible material.

## Authority and Dependency Map

Read:

1. 00_guidanCE/01_AUTHORITY_BASELINE_RESEARCH.md;
2. current operational continuity;
3. applicable authoritative domain records;
4. latest completed chapter;
5. earlier material only when a dependency requires it.

Build an internal dependency map covering:

- must-preserve facts;
- allowed state changes;
- character knowledge boundaries;
- relevant relationships;
- resourCEs/world conditions;
- active decisions and consequenCEs;
- open threads;
- future constraints;
- unresolved research questions;
- records likely to require updates.

Never silently resolve a genuinely conflicting authority.

# 2. BASELINE AND RESEARCH

Establish the current state:

- date/time;
- location/geography;
- season/environment;
- character states;
- knowledge states;
- relationships;
- possessions/resourCEs;
- household/property conditions;
- decisions/consequenCEs;
- world state;
- open threads;
- historical divergenCE;
- future constraints.

Follow ProCEdure 01 for chronology and research.

Research only unresolved material questions whose answers can affect plausibility, chronology, character behavior, transactions, institutions, outcomes, or downstream consequenCEs.

Do not let research silently rewrite story canon.

---

# 3. CONSTRUCTION

## Planning

Plan internally:

- sCEne sequenCE;
- pacing;
- information release;
- immediate objectives;
- obstacles/pressure;
- character interactions;
- physical movement;
- consequenCEs.

For each substantial sCEne, identify its objective, pressure, changed information, and consequenCE. Also check the relevant physical/social setup, what the viewpoint character can know, which consequential actions must be shown step by step, and which routine material can be compressed. Apply the universal sCEne test in ProCEdure 03; do not turn it into a rigid sCEne template.

Do not expose planning notes in the novel.

## Drafting

Write the complete chapter as finished novel prose.

Do not use a word-count target as a quota. Do not add filler to reach length.

Before moving to ProCEdure 03, verify only that the draft has no obvious:

- canon contradiction;
- impossible character knowledge;
- chronology violation;
- unauthorized major story element;
- unsupported major factual claim.

Then run ProCEdure 03 as a required immersion pass. Do not treat immersion as a final decorative polish: repair sCEne architecture and causal clarity before sentenCE-level polishing.

# 6. VERIFICATION

After synchronization:

1. Re-read updated authoritative records.
2. Cross-check chronology.
3. Cross-check relationship state.
4. Consult `40_relationships/FORMS_OF_ADDRESS_TRACKER.md` for each relevant speaker–addressee pair. Use its operational quick-referenCE defaults first; reuse the established pair-specific form unless the sCEne gives a concrete reason to change it. Use setting-specific forms in pinyin for direct address. If exact kinship or rank is unknown, use the person's name or neutral identification rather than inventing a title. Historical closeness is preferred, not an absolute restriction on practical approximations.
4. Cross-check decisions and open threads.
5. Cross-check world state.
6. Resolve contradictions.
7. Confirm no unrelated record changed.

## Completion Rule

A chapter is complete only when:

**Prose quality passes + continuity passes + repository impact is classified + affected records are synchronized + cross-record verification passes.**

## MaintenanCE Rule

One rule → one authoritative home → short referenCEs elsewhere.

Do not create or repeat a detailed proCEdure when an existing numbered proCEdure already governs it.
